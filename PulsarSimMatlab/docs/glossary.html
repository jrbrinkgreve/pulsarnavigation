Glossary

Terms, symbols and variable names used in the code and on this site.

In short

Alphabetical. The three that are easiest to mix up: ρ (snrDB), the pulsar's strength at the pulse peak per hertz; SNR, what a matched filter gets from one pulse or one sub-int; and Bn, the bandwidth that sets the noise of detected power. See SNR and Bn.

adaptive sub-ints (adaptiveSubints)
Short sub-ints merged until each reaches an SNR of about 7. The sub-int length then follows the pulsar's flux, which scintillation changes from hour to hour.
Allan deviation σy(τ)
An oscillator's stability: the typical change of its average fractional frequency from one interval of length τ to the next. White frequency noise makes it fall with τ, flicker keeps it flat, and a random walk makes it grow (makeClock).
Bn, Bnoise
Noise-equivalent bandwidth (∫W²)²/∫W⁴: the bandwidth that sets the variance of detected power. 391.6 MHz full band, 3.0596 MHz per channel.
barycentre (SSB)
The centre of mass of the solar system: the origin of the frame in which a pulsar's timing model holds. SSB stands for solar-system barycentre.
baseline
The off-pulse level of a profile (noise power plus any steady RFI). Fitted, never taken from the truth.
blanking
Setting voltage samples to zero (weight 0) where RFI was detected.
block 1, A–E
The project's work packages: block 1 the full-band chain; A the channelized front end; B RFI excision; C a fast simulator; D motion and phase prediction; E navigation (roadmap).
bulkDelayRef
τ(fref): the dispersion delay of the reference frequency relative to infinite frequency. Recorded, not applied.
channel
One of 128 sub-bands of 3.125 MHz made by channelizeIQ; an ordinary IQ stream at 4.17 MHz.
clock error ε, clock estimate ε̂
ε is the receiver's clock reading minus true time; it shifts every TOA. ε̂ is the observer's own estimate of it, which its predictor uses. Only ε − ε̂ moves the pulse (localisation).
coherent dedispersion
Removing dispersion by applying the inverse chirp filter to the complex voltages: exact at every frequency.
common random numbers
The same random seeds at every point of a sweep, so differences between points come from the swept parameter, not from new noise.
complex envelope / IQ
The complex baseband signal z(t) with x(t) = Re{z·ei2πfLOt}; I and Q are its real and imaginary parts.
Cramér–Rao bound
The smallest variance any unbiased estimator can reach; here the best possible TOA error.
data weights (W, V, X(L))
Per detected bin and channel: valid fraction, variance and lag covariances relative to the radiometer value. From blankingWeights, used by foldProfile 'DataWeights'.
dispersion measure (DM)
Column density of free electrons to the pulsar, in pc cm⁻³; sets the 1/f² delay.
Doppler factor
dΔ/dt: the fractional change of the pulse rate caused by the receiver's motion along the line of sight. Up to 10⁻⁴ from the Earth's orbit, 10⁻⁶ from its rotation.
dutycycle
Pulse FWHM as a percentage of the period (5 % here).
Earth rotation angle (ERA)
The angle by which the Earth has turned, computed from UT1. It turns Earth-fixed positions into the sky-fixed frame.
ECEF
Earth-centred, Earth-fixed: coordinates that turn with the Earth. Sites and ships are given in it (frames).
Einstein delay
TDB − TT: the receiver's clock runs at a varying rate along the Earth's eccentric orbit. ±1.66 ms over a year.
ENU
East, north, up: local axes at a site, used for site offsets (OffsetENU).
ephemeris (ephem)
The observer's timing model: f0, F1, TRef, DM, profileFWHM.
excision
Finding and blanking RFI (block B).
exclusion rule
With equal channel weights: a channel is used in a sub-int only if it has data in every phase bin where any channel has data.
fast simulator (simulateFold)
Draws folded profiles directly from their statistics instead of simulating voltages: hours of data in seconds (block C).
FFTFIT
Taylor's (1992) template-matching TOA method: cross-correlation in the Fourier domain, continuous shifts.
fold, folding
Averaging detected power at equal rotational phase over many turns.
frequency guard
detectRFI blanks strong events (≥ 100× baseline) also in ±7 neighbouring channels, to remove sidelobes below the per-channel threshold.
fullySupported
The sample (or bin) range whose filters saw only real data, e.g. the complete dedispersion sweep. Only this range is folded.
good TOA
Fitted ∧ detected at unknown phase ∧ χ² not flagged.
H0 / H1
The hypotheses "noise only" and "noise + pulse" in the detection test.
half-turn limit
A TOA is unambiguous only while the pulse is less than half a turn from its prediction: ±107,000 km of position error for B0329+54, ±863 km for J0437.
ICRS, J2000
The sky-fixed reference frame of the pulsar directions and the delays: equatorial axes of the year 2000.
INR
Interference-to-noise ratio: RFI power while on, relative to all receiver-noise power in the band.
Isserlis' theorem
Fourth moments of Gaussian variables as sums of products of second moments; gives the covariance of power samples, the root of the noise model.
leftover (L)
In the locked-radar study: folded radar power after excision, in units of the noise baseline.
line of position
Where one TOA puts the station: a plane perpendicular to the pulsar's direction (a line in a 2-D picture). Several cross at the position (physics).
linear assignment
Splitting each time bin between the two nearest phase bins in proportion to distance; unbiased, but correlates neighbours.
LO
Local oscillator, 1.3 GHz here (off-centre on purpose).
MSP
Millisecond pulsar (e.g. J0437−4715).
NoiseCoeffs
Fold option: [V X(1..Lmax)] of correlated time bins (narrow channels, no blanking).
noise ratio
Measured off-pulse variance / modelled variance; 1 if the noise model is right.
Neyman–Pearson (NP)
The most powerful test at a fixed false-alarm rate; for a known shape in Gaussian noise, a matched filter.
optimal weighting
Fit or detection over all channels with inverse-variance weights and one common amplitude (TOA).
oversampled filterbank
A channelizer whose channel rate exceeds the channel width (4/3 here), so aliasing stays out of the useful band.
PFA, PD
False-alarm and detection probabilities.
phase bin
One of NBin (2048) bins of rotational phase.
phase D, phase E
Validation steps of block 1: D the SNR sweep (error bars against SNR), E detection and good TOAs.
phase-locked RFI
RFI whose period is a multiple of the pulsar's, so it folds into the same phases every turn.
polyphase filterbank
An efficient implementation of many mix–filter–decimate channels with one FFT.
power-law clock noise (h₀, h₋₁, h₋₂)
The three coefficients of an oscillator's frequency-noise spectrum: white, flicker and random-walk frequency noise. They set its Allan deviation.
predictor (polyco)
The pulse phase the receiver should see, computed once per observation as short Chebyshev polynomials from the timing model and the delays. The fold only evaluates it (makePredictor). Observatories call these polycos.
PRF
Pulse repetition frequency of a radar (373 Hz for the illustrative one).
Q-Q plot
Sorted normalized errors against Gaussian quantiles; the line y = x means honest error bars.
radiometer equation
Relative noise of averaged power: 1/√(BΔt).
ratio (rms/pred)
rms of actual TOA errors / rms of stated errors; 1 = honest.
reduced χ²
Mean squared normalized fit residual on-pulse; ≫ 1 flags a wrong profile.
RFI
Radio-frequency interference: man-made signals in the band.
RFI profile (rfiProfile)
What RFI and excision leave in each channel's detected power: level, noise and blanked fraction. Measured once on the voltage chain, used by the fast simulator.
Rice factor
√λ₂/2π = 5.51 for this template: the mean number of times per turn that the matched-filter output crosses its mean level upwards. It is not a number of independent trials. At the threshold η = 4.15 the phase search acts like ~60 independent trials; that number grows with η, as ≈ 5.51·η·√(2π).
rise time
Duration of a radar pulse's raised-cosine edge (0.1 µs default).
Roemer delay
The light-travel time across the receiver's offset from the barycentre, along the pulsar's direction. At most ±499 s over a year, and ±21 ms from the Earth's rotation.
sandwich covariance
A⁻¹BA⁻¹: honest parameter covariance when fit weights are not the exact inverse covariance.
scintillation
Fading from multipath interference in the turbulent interstellar plasma.
SEFD
System-equivalent flux density 2kTsys/Aeff: the receiver noise in flux units.
self-noise
The scatter of detected power caused by the pulsar's own noise; proportional to the pulse.
Shapiro delay
The extra travel time of a pulse through the Sun's gravitational field: −17 to +6 µs over a year for B0329+54, about −110 µs when the line of sight grazes the Sun.
SNR, ρ, snrDB
Three different numbers. ρ (snrDB in dB) = Speak/SEFD, a per-hertz ratio set by sky and antenna. SNR per pulse ≈ 388ρ (after matched filtering). SNR per sub-int = SNR per turn × √turns; the design rule is ≥ 6–7.
SSB
Solar-system barycentre; see barycentre.
sub-int (sub-integration)
A group of consecutive turns folded into one profile; one TOA each.
T0, Tmax
Matched-filter statistic at the predicted phase, and its maximum over all phases.
TCB
Barycentric coordinate time. It runs 1.55·10⁻⁸ faster than TDB. A timing model in TCB units must be converted before use with this code.
TDB
Barycentric dynamical time: the time of pulsar timing models and solar-system ephemerides. The code's delays end in TDB.
TECU
Total electron content unit, 10¹⁶ electrons per m²: the unit of the ionosphere's column density (~0.69 ns of delay per TECU at 1.4 GHz).
template
The expected pulse shape (a periodic Gaussian here), peak 1 at phase 0.
timing residual
A TOA minus its predicted value. A position error δr gives −δr·n̂/c, and a clock error adds directly. 1 µs is 300 m (localisation).
TOA
Time of arrival of template phase 0 at the reference frequency (1.6 GHz).
trajectory (makeTrajectory)
Where the receiver is over time: a fixed site, a ship's track, a probe, or later a Kalman filter's estimate. The predictor's only input about the receiver.
TT
Terrestrial time: an ideal clock on the geoid, TAI + 32.184 s. The code counts seconds since J2000.0 in TT.
valid fraction (W)
The fraction of real (not blanked) data in a detected bin after dedispersion spread the blanks.
W50
Pulse width at half maximum (FWHM), as listed in pulsar catalogues.
weight, weight2, weightX
Fold fields: data amount, variance factor and neighbour covariance factor per phase bin.
WGS84
The reference ellipsoid of latitude, longitude and height; makeTrajectory converts it to Earth-fixed coordinates.
