Validation record

Every major measured result against its prediction, in time order, with what each one established. The complete tables are in logs/current_project_notes.md §7.

In short

Every check so far tells the same story. When a sub-integration has an SNR of about 6 or more, the stated TOA error bars are honest to within a few percent, from weak signals to strong ones. The noise model is complete, and the detector's false-alarm rate is as designed. Below that SNR some TOAs lock onto noise peaks, and the good-TOA rule removes them. Interference is the exception: a radar destroys TOAs unless it is excised.

  • Lopsided channels are understood. A flat-channel formula predicted exactly which 8 channels stand out (LTE edges, Inmarsat block edges, GNSS L1) and the 0.39 %. χ² overstates the problem: every channel counts equally in χ², but the TOA fit weights a channel by 1/level², and the lopsided channels contain the RFI, so their weight is small.
  • Worst case: narrow noise-like carriers about as strong as their channel's noise (they keep ~¼ of their weight with ~3.5× the model's noise): χ² 1.22, error bars 3.4 % too small. Enough to matter for navigation → the per-channel noise calibration is worth doing (limits). Done 20 Oct (noiseCal): on the chain (DM 25, 0.2 s) χ² 1.24 → 0.99, σ 3.379 → 3.419 µs.
  • Cost of the environment: −11 % SNR (~25 % longer observing), mostly LTE band 32 (~14 channels effectively lost). The +40 dB radar: all its pulses blanked, none of its energy missed.
  • Bug found in the periodic mask: with periodicMask on (main's default until 9 Oct) the same cases blank 29–32 % of the data (SNR 69 / 66): periodicRFI accepts false emitters at ~14.1 kHz from the dense false flags of the lopsided channels (limits). The mask is off by default since 9 Oct; fix designed, not implemented.

11. New default: DM 25, L 0.2 s (20 Oct)

The default dispersion measure went from 5 to 25 (close to B0329+54's 26.8, the pulsar for the Netherlands scenario) and the run length from 0.1 to 0.2 s, because dedispersion leaves the last sweep of a run (31.5 ms at DM 25) without full support. 12. Algorithm ports and run times

NumPy ports (before MATLAB runs): dispersion kernel group delays exact to 10⁻⁴ µs, leakage ~10⁻¹², overlap-add = direct convolution; forward → IQ → inverse round trip ~2·10⁻⁶; IQ tones ~1.5·10⁻⁶; detection channel mapping + Parseval exact; fold indexing and the nearest/linear bias; FFTFIT Monte Carlo: bias < 0.1 ps noise-free, σ predicted 0.469 vs empirical 0.46–0.47 µs (0.383 without covariance); mixed signal+noise variance formula within 1 %.

Run times (L = 1 s, M-series Mac, 24 GB): dispersion 81.5 s, IQ 46 s, dedispersion 7.4 s, detection 1.2 s, fold 0.05 s; the generator is dominated by random-number generation.
