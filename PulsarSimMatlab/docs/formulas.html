Formulas

The key formulas in one place, each with what it says in words, what every symbol means, and the page where it is derived or used.

In short

A lookup page. Each formula is explained on the linked page; the "In words" column says what it means in one sentence. Two formulas carry most of the project: the radiometer equation (how noisy averaged power is) and Isserlis' theorem behind it (how noise in power samples is correlated). Almost every error bar follows from them.
