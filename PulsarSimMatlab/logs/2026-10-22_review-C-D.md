## Part 2, `simulateFold`: steps 1 and 2 done

**Physics.**
- The fold's statistics are known exactly, so the fold is drawn directly from a Gaussian. There is no voltage data.
- The simulator works in the predictor's frame. All physics of D (geometry, motion, clock) enters through `PhaseOffset`.
- The mean is m_j = 1 + ρ·p̃(φ_j − Δφ). The profile p̃ is smoothed by the time bin (sinc(k·dt·f0)) and by linear
  assignment (sinc²(k/NBin)).
- The noise is var = m²/(B_noise·dt)·weight2/weight². Asymptotic weights are n, 2n/3, n/6 (neighbour correlation 0.25).
  On the channel path they are sums over the triangle-overlap kernel K(s).
- The correlated noise is drawn by FFT: the correlation matrix is circulant, with eigenvalues 1 + 2Σ r_d cos(2πkd/N).

**Checked against first principles:**
- 2n/3 and n/6;
- K(0) = 2/3, and K(1) = 1/6 from both branches;
- the circulant draw;
- the unit-peak normalization (one Gaussian and components);
- both scintillation widths;
- the pass-edge turn formulas.
