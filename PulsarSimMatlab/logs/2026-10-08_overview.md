## In one paragraph

Block A — the **channelized front end** — is finished, validated by your runs and
committed (`27b2367`). `main.m` now runs it by default: the IQ stream is split into 128
channels of 3.125 MHz, each channel is dedispersed and detected, the fold keeps every
channel with its exact noise, and the TOA fit and the detector combine the channels with
**optimal weights** (each channel and bin weighted by its noise). Blanked (cut-out) data
is handled exactly: a blanking mask is turned into exact data weights, and the profile
stays unbiased. What is missing is the excision itself — finding the RFI and blanking it.
That is block **B**, next.

## Why block A was needed (reminder)

At the worst-case design point the pulsar is ~54 dB below the noise: one radar pulse
outweighs ~80,000 pulsar pulses. RFI must therefore be **cut out (blanked)** before
dedispersion smears it over milliseconds — per channel, because most RFI is narrowband.
Every later stage must then know how much valid data went into each point and what its
exact noise is. Block A built exactly that chain.

## Insights worth keeping

1. **Weak-signal requirement for blanking.** The pulsar is ~4e-6 of the noise level, so
   any blanking error in the mean profile that **depends on pulse phase** must be far
   below 4e-6 of the baseline. Errors that are the same at all phases only shift the
   baseline and are harmless. Blanking depends on pulse phase only if it is locked to the
   pulsar's (barycentric) phase; topocentric RFI drifts against it (Doppler). This is why
   the mean model was made exact (true channel spectrum).
2. **No simple rule for the noise of blanked data** (neither ∝ W nor ∝ W²; errors up to
   ±45 %); the exact calculation from the mask is cheap and is what `blankingWeights` does.
3. **Known channel gains, not free amplitudes per channel.** At −54 dB each channel's pulse
   has SNR ≪ 1 per sub-int; 128 free amplitudes would fit noise. The optimal fit uses one
   amplitude × known relative gain s_c (default flat; pulsar spectrum × bandpass).
4. **Testing lesson.** Averages can hide effects: the fold bug was invisible in the
   all-channel mean (2.3σ) and obvious per blanking type (7σ). Tests now check per group.
   And inside a blank neighbouring bins share data — use the scatter between channels for
   uncertainties, not bin counts (an earlier "~4 % deficit" claim was over-stated: −4 ± 4 %).

## Next: block B (RFI excision) — what it needs

1. **Blanking of channel files** (new function): zero the masked samples before
   `dedisperseChannels` (the A3a test does this inline; B makes it a function).
2. **RFI detection per channel** (one function at a time): robust power threshold on
   short windows (median/MAD), spectral kurtosis, whole-channel flags for persistent
   narrowband RFI. Tune the **false-flag rate on clean data**. Careful: the −5 dB test
   pulsar is bright enough to trigger a detector — test at weak SNR too; at −54 dB the
   pulsar can never trigger it.
3. **Realistic L-band scenario**: rotating ATC radar (new gating option in `rfiSource`),
   a harmonic-PRF radar, GNSS L1/L2/E6, Inmarsat, LTE (frequencies to verify).
4. **Hook in `main.m`** (channel path): mask → blank channel files → dedisperse → detect →
   `blankingWeights` → `foldProfile(..., 'DataWeights', ...)` → 'optimal' TOAs/detection.
5. **Characterize per RFI type**: flagged fraction, residual noise ratio, TOA ratio and χ²,
   compared with the 5 Oct no-excision table (§7).

```matlab
run('tests/testChannelizeIQ.m')        % ~10 s
run('tests/testDedisperseChannels.m')  % ~15 s
run('tests/testDetectChannels.m')      % ~1 s
run('tests/testFoldWeights.m')         % ~7 s
run('tests/testChannelTOA.m')          % ~5 s
run('tests/testBlankingWeights.m')     % ~66 s
run('tests/testOptimalTOA.m')          % ~130 s (Monte Carlo)
```
`main.m`: `frontEnd` and `weighting` are in `pipelineParams.m`. Channel mode skips the
iq / detected / fold check plots (they are built for the full band); the TOA validation
plot works. With L = 0.1 s the data files take ~7 GB. The files in `data/chan/` are
shared by `main.m` and the tests (seed 43, regenerated identically); the regression
references are `data/mc/foldRef_pre3c.mat` and `data/mc/toaRef_preA2.mat`.
