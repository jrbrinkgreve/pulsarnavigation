  means of the exact variance on a 6000-bin window with measurements over all bins →
  two DM 100 groups failed: a comparison artefact (different bins, steep variance vs W
  inside a group). Fixed by computing the exact covariance for every bin via mask
  convolutions (C_{a,a+τ} = (q_τ ∗ keep)(a), q_τ = h·h*(·+τ)), cross-checked with the
  direct matrix (≤ 7e-8). Result: PASS; measured/exact = 1 ± 0.002 in all six cases;
  neither g(w) = w² nor w holds (errors up to ±45 %); next-bin covariance at blanked
  edges ≈ 0.05·var0, not w²-scaled.
- Design consequence: replace the g(w) idea by exact per-time-bin noise statistics
  (variance and lag covariances), computed from the mask where one exists (step 2, needs
  the channel's dedispersion kernel h) and constant from the channel spectrum otherwise
  (unit 3). Revised sub-units proposed to Jasper.
