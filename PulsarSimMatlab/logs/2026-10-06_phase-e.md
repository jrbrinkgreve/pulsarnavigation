## Later: long H0 run (`runH0.m`, new script)

Design agreed: separate script (not an option in `runSNRSweep`); 100 noise-only passes on
a zero sky with new noise seeds (`seed + 10000 + i`); T0/Tmax statistics, exceedances at
P_FA 1e-1/1e-2/1e-3, and the bin-bin correlation of `normProfile` with the T0 std it
implies. Committed after phase E (42e85e9 had phase E).

ratio 0.996; lag-1 correlation 0.2484 vs model 0.2501, lags 2–200 consistent with 0
(mean −0.0008 = mean-subtraction effect, max 0.0026); implied T0 std 0.997; exceedances
T0 96/7/3 and Tmax 88/13/1 (expected 90/9/0.9).
