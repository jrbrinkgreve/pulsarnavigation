**The expSimRFI stop.**
- `expSimVsChain`, `expSimRFI` and `expComponentsChain` are scripts. They share the base workspace, and each
  does `oldDir = cd(root); restoreDir = onCleanup(@() cd(oldDir))`.
- `run()` starts a script in its own folder (tests/). So expSimVsChain's `restoreDir` stays in the workspace
  and holds oldDir = tests/.

  to tests/ right after `cd(root)`. `pipelineParams` (in root) is then not found.
- Not a fault of expSimRFI's own code.
- Workaround: `clear` before each of these scripts.
- Proposed fix (one line in each of the three scripts): `clear restoreDir` before `oldDir = cd(root)`.

## A-1 fixed (Jasper: "okay, go ahead and fix it")

**Physics.** A merged sub-int's tRef is the time of its middle turn, and TOA = tRef + Δφ/fRef. With a predictor
the turn → time mapping holds the Doppler shift and the delays. `mergeSubints` used the f0 model for every fold.
For a predictor fold, f0 only labels the fold, and TRef = Phi0 = 0. The merged tag was therefore off by
≈ (v·n̂/c)·t + frac0/f0: 0.3 s per hour of recording for B0329+54.

**Change (functions/mergeSubints.m).**
- The fold has no predictor (`info_fold.predictor` empty): the f0 / F1 formula, unchanged. Every current
  result stays bit-identical.
- A predictor fold: tRef = tRef_m + (turnRef − turnRef_m)/fRef_m and fRef = fRef_m, from the member sub-int
  m that holds turnRef. `foldProfile` solved that member on the predictor. The error left is
  ½(ḟ/f)Δt²: ~45 ns for 60 s sub-ints on the Earth.
- A group whose turnRef falls in a gap between its members is refused (`mergeSubints:predictor`). It cannot
  occur within one recording, whose sub-ints are contiguous.
- The help header explains this.

**Test (tests/testFoldPredictor.m, new test 6).** The probe of tests 1–3 (Doppler 1e-3): the 100-turn
predictor fold merged in pairs, against the 200-turn predictor fold. The middle sub-int is left out for the
gap check.
