## C3b: scintillation (literature first, then code; Jasper: "go with your recommendations")

Literature (web search, 20 Oct): J0437 at 1.4 GHz — Δν_d ≈ 740 MHz, Δt_d ≈ 2290 s (IAR 2019–2020
campaign, arXiv:2010.00010, Sec. III.2, rescaled from Keith et al. 2013 at 1.5 GHz); Gwinn et al.
2006 found two scales at 327 MHz, rescaled to 1.4 GHz ~5727 s and ~515 s; "those scintillation
scales have been observed to vary notably between epochs"; refractive factors 2–3 (MWA, arXiv:
1407.4745); scaling Δν_d ∝ ν^4.4 (Kolmogorov; ν^3.9 measured), Δt_d ∝ ν^1.2. → at 1.4 GHz our
400 MHz band holds about one scintle: J0437's band-averaged flux is fully modulated, every ~40 min.

simulateFold: 'FluxScale' (given F) or 'Scintillation' (struct: bandwidth HWHM [Hz], timescale 1/e
[s], band): local scintField — white complex noise on a padded time (step Δt_d/10) × frequency
(≥ 64 points) grid, separable Gaussian kernels (std σ/√2, unit power), F = |g|², linear
interpolation at sub-int mid times, mean over each channel's points; own RandStream (mrg32k3a)
→ noise draws unchanged; warning if sub-ints > Δt_d/5. truth.fluxScale. C3a modes bit-identical
(fold, info, truth for plain, RFI and schedule cases).
