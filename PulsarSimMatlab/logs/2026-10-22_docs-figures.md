## The delayModel port

`docs/tools/figures/delaymodel.py` ports `delayModel`'s analytic branch (Keplerian EMB, Jupiter and Saturn, the lunar series, the
Earth rotation angle, the two-term Einstein delay, Shapiro) and makeTrajectory's `'fixed'` and linear `'track'` types. It reproduces
the tests: daily Roemer amplitude 7.607 ms at 52° N, Shapiro −17.1 to +5.8 µs, the 100 km east sine 193.3 µs, and
testMakePredictor test 5 exactly (15 segments, the shortest 7.03 s, every check within 1 ns).
