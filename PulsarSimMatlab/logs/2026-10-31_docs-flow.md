## The rules applied
- each paragraph opens from the one before; each section ends on what the next one answers, and the next opens by answering it;
- no bold-label openers ("In the code:", "Why NBin 2048.", "Design for the worst case.") — the label becomes a topic sentence;
- lists that carry an argument (findings, consequences, examples) become paragraphs; procedures, catalogues and tables stay;
- the reason comes before the mechanism;
- no dates or run provenance in running text or headings: they move into a collapsed "History" or "Runs" box;
- reference pages (parameters, formulas, glossary, tests, history, maintenance) get openers and bridges only;
- api.html is generated from the MATLAB help texts and is not touched.

Every page is checked against HEAD with a token diff (numbers, code names, links, ids): nothing may be lost.
