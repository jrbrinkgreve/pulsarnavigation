## Hardware facts from Jasper (21 Oct)
- Antenna, then a mixer, then an SDR.
- 18 SDR channels: one per element and polarisation (digital beamforming possible).
- 400 MHz instantaneous bandwidth (the simulator's and the scenario budgets' B).
- Mixer separate from the SDR or the SDR's built-in one: not known yet.

## Design choices in the writeup (proposals, not decisions)
- High-side LO at 2.0 GHz, IF 0.4-0.8 GHz (inverted), one LO split to all 18 mixers. RF filter rejects
  0.4-0.8 GHz (direct leak to the IF) and 2.4-2.8 GHz (image). If the mixer is the SDR's own: LO at band
  centre 1.4 GHz, leakage in the protected 1400-1427 MHz band, I/Q imbalance -25 to -40 dB before correction.
- One 10 MHz + 1 PPS reference for LO and SDRs; the SDRs' own +-2 ppm crystals unused; OCXO or Rb for J0437.
- Full digital beamforming per channel after the channeliser (true time delay to 0.03 rad per 3.125 MHz
  channel), attitude-steered on top of the steered mount; GNSS satellites as phase calibrators.
- Detection pipeline = the existing processIQ chain + predictor fold + adaptiveSubints + detectPulsar.

## Relevance to the simulator (for later, not acted on)
The simulation's LO (1.3 GHz) lies inside the band (limits.html). With a real mixer, either the LO plan
above (outside the band, inverted IF) or I/Q imbalance and LO leakage at the band centre would need to be
modelled before real data go into processIQ.
